Help & Support

We can help with playback, accounts, subscriptions, payments and downloads.

Contact support

Email: NollyBoxng@gmail.com

WhatsApp: Chat with NollyBox support (+234 812 887 3450)

When contacting us, describe the issue and include the email used for your NollyBox account or payment reference when relevant. Never send your password, card PIN or one-time verification code.

Playback problems

  • Check your internet connection and retry.
  • Close and reopen NollyBox, then try the title again.
  • For offline playback, confirm the download is complete and the account or subscription still has access to the title.

Payment or Premium access

Allow a short time for payment verification. If payment was successful but Premium is still inactive, contact us with your payment reference, amount and approximate time. If checkout was cancelled or payment failed, Premium should not be activated for that transaction. Please do not share full card details.

Refund requests

Contact support with the payment reference, date, amount and reason for the request. We review requests individually and follow applicable law and payment-provider requirements. Duplicate charges or billing errors should be reported promptly. A request is not confirmed until support replies.

Account and privacy requests

For help accessing your account, correcting profile information or making a privacy request, email us from the address associated with your account where possible.
