Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how NollyBox handles information when you use our website or app. We aim to collect only information needed to provide, secure and improve the service.

Information we may handle

  • Account information: email address and authentication details managed by our authentication provider, plus profile information you choose to provide, such as a display name or avatar.
  • Service activity: information needed to provide playback, downloads, saved titles, watch progress and account support. Some preferences and viewing progress may be stored on your device.
  • Subscription and payment records: plan, payment reference, amount, currency, status and subscription dates needed to verify payment and manage Premium access.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, network or diagnostic information that may be processed when necessary to operate, protect or troubleshoot the service.
  • Messages to support: information you include when contacting us.

How information is used

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide the catalogue and playback, manage Premium subscriptions, verify payments, respond to requests, prevent fraud or abuse, troubleshoot errors and meet applicable legal obligations.

Payments

Payments are processed through Paystack. NollyBox does not intend to store your full card number or card security code. Paystack and relevant payment providers process payment information under their own terms and privacy notices. We receive transaction information needed to confirm payment and manage your subscription.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers for functions such as hosting, authentication, database/storage and payment processing. Information may be shared with them only as needed to provide or protect NollyBox, or where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained while your account or subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for security, dispute resolution, accounting or legal purposes.

Your choices and requests

You can choose what profile details to provide and may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information associated with your account. Some records may need to be retained where lawfully required or necessary to resolve disputes and protect the service.

Children

NollyBox is not intended for children to create accounts or make purchases without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. Guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.
